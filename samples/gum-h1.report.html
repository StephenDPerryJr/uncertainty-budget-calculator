Method

Uncertainty evaluated per JCGM 100:2008 (GUM) and NIST TN 1297: standard uncertainties from Type A (statistical) and Type B evaluations, combined by the law of propagation of uncertainty (first-order Taylor series, GUM 5.1.2 / 5.2.2), effective degrees of freedom by the Welch–Satterthwaite formula (GUM G.4.1), coverage factor from the Student t-distribution (GUM G.3/G.6.4), and reported to two significant digits (GUM 7.2.6). Rounding: rounded to nearest.
